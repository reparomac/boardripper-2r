Ao atualizar o BoardRipper, reaplicar no index-*.js:
  const XV=ID()==="es"?{...UV,...WV}:ID()==="en"?VV:HV
→ const XV=ID()==="es"?{...UV,...WV,...(window.__2rTrEs||{})}:ID()==="en"?{...VV,...(window.__2rTrEn||{})}:HV
O app injeta window.__2rTrEn / window.__2rTrEs (data/ui/boardripper_tr.json) em ui/boardview_view.py.
